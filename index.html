Title IX & NIL Research

Ask about gender equity in college athletics: who gets to play, what schools spend on men's and women's teams, and how revenue sharing and NIL are changing the picture.

About

What it explores
Federal Equity in Athletics (EADA) filings for every co-ed college with intercollegiate athletics, a curated table of NIL and revenue-sharing figures from published reports, primary Title IX and settlement documents, and recent news.
Who it's for
Student journalists, athletes and recruits, Title IX coordinators, and advocates.
How to use it
Name the schools, conference, sport or years you care about. A lead agent splits your question, data, news and policy researchers work in parallel, and a writer reports only findings whose evidence was checked against the source. Every sentence cites a source, every number has a date, and the report says what the data can't answer. The panel on the right shows each agent's context.
